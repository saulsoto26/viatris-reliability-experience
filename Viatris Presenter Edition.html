Produce capsules that consistently meet approved quality specifications, under safe and controlled operating conditions.

Six possible explanations, none yet proven

The question is not why it failed after the PM. It is whether the PM contains a task that can detect or prevent this mechanism.

H1

The PM tasks do not address the mechanism behind capsule loss.

H2

The task addresses it, but its method, interval or acceptance criteria are insufficient.

H3

The failure is random or not age-related, so time-based tasks cannot control it.

H4

The origin is outside the suspected subsystem: capsule supply, size change or set-up.

H5

Operating conditions changed: product mix, format, speed or shifts.

H6

Several orders describe the same event, so recurrence is overstated.

Each hypothesis leads to a different strategy. That is why this is an engineering question, not a judgment of the maintenance team.
