Two capsule fillers, two histories

C-8 (MG2 G-250) and C-11 (IMA): different machines, different stories. Neither number is a failure rate.

C-8 · Understand and sustain

76 to 37 orders from 2024 to 2025, but mostly the end of a 2024 peak. Per month: 4.56 before the PM change to CFI022M1, 4.06 after (−11%). 2026 is rising again.

C-11 · Investigate and optimize

46 to 62 orders. The largest single increase is orders we cannot classify (+11). The clearest functional signal is dosing: 36 weight-variation and granulation-loss orders.
